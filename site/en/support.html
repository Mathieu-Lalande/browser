Support

Need help?

Answers to frequent questions about Browser for desktop, iPhone, iPad and Android, and how to reach us.

Contact us

  • Report a problem or suggest an idea: GitHub issues (include the device, the system and the version of Browser, shown in Settings, About).
  • The user guide describes every feature step by step.
  • Privacy: see the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sync the computer and the phone?

Turn on sync on the computer (Settings, Sync) with your server, show the pairing code, then scan it from the phone (Settings, Sync). Details in the guide.

How do I make Browser the default browser?

Android: phone Settings, Apps, Default apps, Browser app, then Browser. iPhone and iPad: Settings, Apps, Browser, Default Browser App (when the installed version allows it). Computer: Browser's Settings, Default browser.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No, unless you turn on sync (end-to-end encrypted, to your server), the AI assistant or translation (with your key). See the privacy policy.

I lost the pairing code or a device

No one can recover the key: the data is encrypted with it. In Settings, Sync, choose “Reset” on a device that still has the data, then pair the others again.

How do I change the language?

Browser follows your system's language (French or English, otherwise English). To choose: Settings, Appearance, Language, on the computer as on the phone.
