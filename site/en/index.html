Features

Designed to keep track

Spaces and gestures

One space per context

Work, studies, leisure: each space keeps its tabs, its icon and its color. Go from one to the other with a two-finger swipe on the touchpad, or a finger on a touch screen.

  • Pinned tabs and a vertical sidebar
  • Isolated space: separate cookies and sign-ins, like a container
  • Ctrl Alt 1…9 to jump from one space to another
Split view

Two to four pages side by side

Read documentation while coding, compare two product pages or take notes during a video: split view shows up to four tabs in the same window.

  • From a tab's menu or Ctrl Shift \
  • Adjustable dividers, layout restored at startup
Universal bar

The whole browser under Ctrl K

Tabs of every space, bookmarks, history, actions and settings in one fuzzy search. It calculates, converts units and gives the time in a city, without sending anything online.

  • Filters @tabs, @bookmarks, @history…
  • Tabs from your other devices
Reading

Read without distraction

Reader mode keeps only the article: text, links and images, in the font, size and theme of your choice. Curious about a link? Its preview opens in a card, without losing your place.

  • Ctrl Alt R to enter and exit reader mode
  • Shift + click on a link for its preview
Folders, favorites, archiving

Tidy up, find again, forget the rest

Sort your tabs into folders with drag and drop, keep your everyday sites in the favorites grid, and let Browser archive the tabs you no longer open.

  • Nested, collapsible folders with an icon
  • Archived tabs found again in browser://archive
Themes

Your colors, everywhere

Light, dark or automatic theme, an accent color with an always readable palette, animated backgrounds, density, corner radius, font. Pick from the gallery or share your theme as a code.

  • Share as a code, a .browsertheme file or a QR code
  • Zen mode: the page alone, edge to edge

And everything else

Every detail, already there

No extension to hunt for the essentials: everything is built in, and can be turned off.

  • Universal bar

    Calculations, conversions (length, mass, temperature, data…), time zones and bases, answer copied with Enter. Open beside with Shift Enter, in the background with Alt Enter.

  • Link preview

    Shift + click on a link: the page opens in a floating card, to keep as a tab or in split view.

  • Reader mode

    The article alone, without the rest of the page. Ctrl Alt R.

  • Boosts

    Customize a site: colors, font, elements hidden with a click, CSS, an optional script.

  • Passwords

    Saved in a vault encrypted by the system, filled on the right site only, CSV export.

  • Extensions

    Install Chrome extensions from a folder, a .zip or a .crx.

  • Screenshots and video

    Visible area, full page or an element picked with the mouse; video recording of the tab, sound included. Ctrl Shift S, Ctrl Alt V.

  • Page options

    Zoom, screenshot, mobile site, boost, the page's QR code: all at the end of the address bar.

  • Complete menus

    The app's “…” menu, right-click on the sidebar, spaces and pages; compact mode as a rail of icons.

  • Gestures

    Pinch to zoom, two-finger swipe to go back, Ctrl + click to select several tabs.

  • Send to a device

    A tab from the PC to the phone (and back), end-to-end encrypted, expired after 7 days.

  • Your own shortcuts

    Change or remove the shortcut of each command; conflicts are flagged.

  • Theme gallery

    Eight ready-to-use themes, and yours to share as a code or a QR code.

  • Automatic updates

    Downloaded in the background, installed at the next restart. Stable or beta channel.

  • Encrypted sync

    Bookmarks, tabs, recent history, spaces, theme and boosts, through your own server.

  • Private windows

    An in-memory session per window, erased when it closes. Ctrl Shift N.

  • Shields

    Ad and tracker blocker, HTTPS only, pop-ups blocked, each can be turned off per site.

  • Sleeping tabs

    Inactive tabs free their memory, never during a video or a form in progress.

  • Downloads

    No dialog, with pause and resume; programs wait for your confirmation.

  • Find and zoom

    Find in page, zoom remembered per site, picture in picture, sound muted tab by tab.

  • Mobile app

    Address bar at the bottom, swipe between tabs, spaces, reader mode, passwords, and your PC's tabs thanks to sync.

Privacy

Private by default, not as an option

Protections are on from the first launch. Nothing to install, nothing to set up.

  • Built-in blocker

    Ads and trackers blocked with the EasyList and uBlock lists, a counter per tab.

  • HTTPS only

    Every address goes to https; if a site doesn't allow it, Browser asks you first.

  • Private windows

    No history, no cookies, no password offers, nothing synced.

  • End-to-end encrypted

    Sync goes through your server, which only sees unreadable data.

  • Less fingerprinting

    Global Privacy Control, a browser identity without any trace of Electron, a language that can be standardized.

  • Pop-ups

    A page can only open a window after a real click from you.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”. Is that normal?

Yes. Versions aren't signed with a developer certificate yet, and SmartScreen warns about any program that is rarely downloaded. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. You can check the file with the SHA-256 checksums published with each version.

macOS refuses to open the app.

The app isn't notarized by Apple yet. Right-click Browser in the Applications folder, then “Open”, or allow it in System Settings, Privacy & Security, “Open Anyway”.

Do I need administrator rights to install it?

No. The Windows installer offers to install Browser for you alone, without administrator rights, or for all users. It creates a Start menu shortcut, a desktop shortcut if you want one, and uninstalls from Windows Settings.

How does Browser update?

By itself: it checks for new versions 30 seconds after startup and then every 6 hours, downloads them in the background and installs them at the next restart (or right away with “Restart to update”). The portable .zip version, the .deb package and, as long as the app isn't signed, macOS are updated by hand.

What does Browser do with my data?

Your history, bookmarks, passwords and sessions stay on your computer. Search goes through DuckDuckGo by default, trackers are blocked and private browsing leaves no trace. Nothing is sent anywhere else, unless you turn on sync, and it is encrypted.

How does sync work? Who hosts the server?

You do. Sync goes through a small server you deploy (Docker, Fly.io, Render…), and data is encrypted on your devices before sending: the server never sees plain text. Private browsing is never synced. See the guide.

Do my Chrome extensions work?

Partly: Browser loads unpacked extensions and .zip or .crx files with their content scripts and part of the chrome.* APIs, but without action buttons or extension pop-ups. The blocker, passwords and reader mode are already built in.
