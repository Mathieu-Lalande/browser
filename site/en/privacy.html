Privacy

Privacy policy

Browser collects no data. This page describes, for the computer, iPhone, iPad and Android, what stays on your device and what only leaves it if you decide so.

In short

  • No account, no ads, no trackers, no usage analytics, no crash report sent automatically.
  • The developer receives, stores and sells no data about you.
  • Your history, tabs, bookmarks, notes, passwords and settings stay on your device.
  • Features that contact a third-party service are optional, off by default, and use the server or key you provide.

Last updated: November 3, 2026.

What stays on your device

History, tabs, spaces, bookmarks, notes and highlights, boosts, themes and settings are saved locally. Passwords and form data are encrypted by the system (iOS keychain, Android Keystore, the computer's secure storage). Private tabs and windows save nothing.

The pages you visit receive what any browser sends them (IP address, headers): how they handle it is up to each site's policy.

Optional features

Sync

Off by default. It goes through a server you deploy yourself. Data is encrypted on the device (XChaCha20-Poly1305) before sending: the server never sees plain text. Nothing private is synced. QR-code pairing uses the camera, only while scanning.

AI assistant

Off by default. If you turn it on with your own Anthropic key, the page's text and your question are sent to Anthropic, only when you ask. Their handling is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Translation

Off by default. If you choose a service (DeepL, LibreTranslate or Google) with your key, the page's text is sent to it when you ask for a translation.

New tab weather

If you add the weather widget, the name of the chosen city is sent to Open-Meteo, with no key or cookie.

Desktop only

Browser checks for updates on GitHub (download of the release files, nothing about you). Crash reports stay on the computer; they are only sent if you tick “Send crash reports” (off by default). The mobile app sends no reports.

Your rights and contact

Since no data is collected, there is nothing to access or delete on our side: uninstalling the app erases its data; synced data lives on your own server. These apps are meant for adults (unfiltered Internet access).

Questions: open a GitHub issue or see the Support page. Any change to this policy will be published on this page.
